# Data Structure Course for Beginners 🚀  

## 📌 Overview  
This guide is designed to help you effectively start your problem-solving journey on LeetCode. By following these steps and strategies, you can gradually build confidence, improve your problem-solving skills, and reach 100+ solved problems efficiently.  

---

## 🌟 Key Considerations  

### 1. Start Small  
- Begin with the **easiest problems** to build momentum and confidence.  
- Avoid jumping into complex problems before mastering the basics.  

### 2. Learn, Then Solve  
- Focus on understanding how different problems are solved rather than struggling to solve everything on your own initially.  
- Watch tutorials, read explanations, or study other people's solutions to grasp new concepts.  

### 3. Time Management  
- Spend **30–45 minutes** on average for each problem.  
- If a problem takes **more than 1 hour**, move on and revisit it later. Add it to your backlog for practice.  
- Aim for **speed and efficiency** in problem-solving to maximize your learning in less time.  

### 4. Balanced Practice  
- Avoid solving too many problems from a single topic in one go.  
- Solve **5–10 problems per topic** to diversify your understanding and exposure to different types of problems.  

### 5. Master the Prerequisites 
Before attempting problems, ensure you understand the required concepts. For example:  
- **HashSet/HashMap**: Learn how they work before solving problems related to them.  
- **Recursion**: Study recursion thoroughly before diving into recursive problems.  
- **Graphs/Trees**: Understand graph and tree basics before solving related problems.  
- **Dynamic Programming**: Build a solid foundation in DP before attempting DP-related problems.  

---

## 🎯 Goals  
1. Build a strong foundation in data structures and algorithms.  
2. Solve problems across diverse topics.  
3. Develop efficient time-management skills.  
4. Achieve a milestone of solving **100+ problems** on LeetCode.  

---

## 📝 Notes  
- Stay consistent and track your progress.  
- Keep revisiting previously solved problems to reinforce concepts.  
- Don't hesitate to learn from the community or mentors.  
- Enjoy the process—problem-solving is a skill that improves over time with dedication and practice!  

Happy coding! 🚀
